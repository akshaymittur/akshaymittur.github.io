PRIVACY & COOKIES

Privacy & cookies.

This is Akshay Mittur’s personal portfolio, hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no accounts or contact forms. Email links open your own email application.

Optional website analytics

When configured, this site uses Google Analytics 4 only after you opt in. If you decline, or no measurement ID has been configured, the site does not load the Google Analytics tag or send analytics events.

Analytics can measure page visits, referral sources, device and browser information, approximate location, and interactions such as resume links, project story views, and contact clicks. It does not identify individual visitors to me. I do not send your name, email address, or contact-message content to Analytics. Website analytics should not be used to identify individual visitors.

Your choices

  • Analytics only: allows analytics cookies and measurement, with advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization denied.
  • Allow all: also permits the advertising-related consent signals used by Google for features such as aggregate demographics, if those features are enabled in the Analytics property.
  • Decline: no Google Analytics tag is loaded on future page loads.

Your selection is stored locally in your browser under am-analytics-consent-v1. You can change it at any time using . Withdrawing consent stops future collection after reload; it does not delete data previously collected by Google or automatically remove previously set Google cookies. You may remove those cookies in your browser settings.

Demographics and retention

Age, gender, and interests are not available for every visitor. Availability depends on Google’s eligibility rules, the property’s configuration, user consent, and minimum reporting thresholds. Google Analytics may apply privacy thresholds to small audiences.

Analytics data retention is controlled in the Google Analytics property. Google may process data outside your country. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services or use Google’s analytics opt-out tool.

Hosting and external links

GitHub may process technical request information when serving this website, independently of optional analytics. See GitHub’s privacy statement. LinkedIn, GitHub, and other external websites have their own privacy policies. Fonts are served directly from this site.
